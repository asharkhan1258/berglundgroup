Business Litigation

Business Litigation Services & Fees

Business litigation is inherently unpredictable — no two disputes unfold the same way. Rather than a single flat fee for an entire case, we structure our engagements in phases, giving you cost certainty where possible and flexibility where the matter demands it.

Hourly Rates

Rates are based on attorney experience and case complexity.

Attorney Level Hourly Rate
Associate Attorney $350 – $450/hr
Senior Associate / Of Counsel $450 – $650/hr
Partner $650 – $950/hr

Rates are provided as a general guide. Final rates depend on case complexity, urgency, and scope, and are confirmed in your engagement letter.
