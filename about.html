About Us

Our Story

In 2019, Keith Berglund established a law firm - The BG Service (“TBG”) - based in Los Angeles to provide a large firm experience and results with small firm value and engagement. Since then, other talented attorneys have joined TBG to help TBG serve the needs of our clients with the highest level of care.

Our Mission

At The BG Service, clients come first. Whether it is helping a client achieve their business goals or overcoming legal challenges, our principal objective is to provide the highest quality services available anywhere and to be an instrumental part of each client’s success. With over 40 years of legal experience and working at some of the largest law firms in the world, The BG Service provides a large firm experience and results with small firm value and engagement. We provide our clients the personal attention of our highly experienced lawyers to achieve first-rate results. With satisfied clients throughout the United States and the World, The BG Service is routinely retained to take on lawsuits and problems that call for a sophisticated understanding of complex legal issues. If you are seeking skilled and qualified representation with a focused and client-friendly environment, contact The BG Service to schedule a free consultation.
